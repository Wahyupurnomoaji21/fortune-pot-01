One kind, funny fortune (113 characters) for the houseplant on the
windowsill, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> On Thursday, the morning sun will pause at your leaves and stay for tea — your windowsill is its favourite table.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/mf_fp1 node main.ts
cat /tmp/mf_fp1/entry.txt
```
